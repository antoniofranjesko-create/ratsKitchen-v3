# RAT'S KITCHEN
### Rules — v4.1.0

**3–8 players** (best at 3–5) · **20–40 minutes**

You run an unlicensed food stall in the night market. So does everyone else. You all have rats.

The Health Inspector is coming. The only question is whose kitchen he finds first.

> Where this document and the code disagree, the code is right.

---

## HOW YOU WIN

Two ways. Pick one, or switch when the table turns.

**RAT KING** — Start your turn with **6 total rat weight** in your kitchen.
Reaching 6 isn't enough. You have to still be holding it when your next turn begins, so everyone gets one round to knock you down.

**LAST STALL STANDING** — Everyone else loses all their stars.
Survive. Let the inspectors do the work.

---

## SETUP

The setup is the same at every player count.

1. Each player takes a **kitchen** (a play area in front of them) and **4 stars**.
2. Separate the **rats** from the action cards. Set the 4 Ratato and 4 Hot Ratato aside — they always go in the deck.
3. Shuffle the other 39 rats. Deal **18 into the deck**. The other 21 go to **the bins**.
4. Shuffle the deck (action cards + its 18 rats + the 8 Ratato/Hot Ratato).
5. Turn **3 rats face-up** from the bins. **This is the alley.**
6. Deal each player **1 Food** plus **6 more cards** from the deck.
   *During setup only: any rat dealt goes to the bins, any Snap Inspection is set aside and shuffled back in.*
7. Put the **6 Market Day cards** in a face-up pile of their own.
8. Give the starting player the **first-player marker**.

---

## THE TABLE

- **Your kitchen** is public. Everyone sees your rats, always.
- **Your hand** is the only thing you keep secret.
- **The bins** are a face-up pile of rats out of play.
- **The alley** is 3 rats face-up beside the bins. Refill it from the bins the moment one is taken.

---

## PLAYING A ROUND

### Start of round
Flip **one Market Day card**. It applies to everyone for the whole round. Discard it at the end of the round.

*A round is one pass around the table. When play returns to the first-player marker, flip the next Market Day.*

### Your turn

**1. Rat King check**
If you have 6 or more rat weight, you win.

**2. Resolve your own placed cards**
Anything you placed last turn happens now — Hot Chilli explodes, Territorial expires.

**3. Your armed inspection ages**
If you have one armed, this is the turn to fire it. It expires at the end of this turn.

**4. Draw one card**

> **The card you draw might not reach your hand.**
> - **Action card** → your hand
> - **Rat** → straight into your kitchen (a Hot Ratato goes to your hand instead)
> - **Snap Inspection** → it fires on *you*, right now, then discards

**5. Play as many cards as you like**
No limit. But **each rival kitchen can only be touched once per turn** — poach from someone and you can't also inspect them.

*Put a chip on each kitchen you touch. Sweep them back when your turn ends.*

**6. Discard down to 7 cards. Turn passes left.**

## RATS

Every rat has two numbers:

- **WEIGHT** — counts toward Rat King (you need 6)
- **HEAT** — damage you take when an inspection matches its colour

Every rat also has a **colour**: red, blue or green.

### The rats

| Rat | Copies | W | H | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Street Rat** | 19 | 1 | 1 | Plain stock |
| **Fat Rat** | 7 | 2 | 2 | Heavy. Cannot be shielded by a Cat |
| **Runner Rat** | 3 | 1 | 1 | **Feed 1 Food: draw a card** |
| **Sow Rat** | 3 | 2 | 2 | **Feed 1 Food: draw 2 cards** |
| **Larder Rat** | 3 | 1 | 1 | **Feed 1 Food: eat it, regain 1 star** |
| **Breeder Rat** | 2 | 1 | 1 | **Free, once a turn: take any rat from the alley** |
| **Sewer Rat** | 2 | 1 | **0** | **Colourless** — no inspection can ever touch it |
| **Ratato Rat** | 4 | 0 | 1 | Worthless, still smells. Goes into *your* kitchen when drawn |
| **Hot Ratato** | 4 | 0 | 1 | Held in hand — throw it into a **rival's** kitchen |

**47 rats total.**

### Feeding

Runner, Sow and Larder have a **Feed** ability: discard 1 Food from your hand, use the ability. Breeder's ability is free.

**Once per turn per rat** — turn the rat sideways when you use it, straighten it at the start of your next turn.

Your own cards can bring only **one rat into your own kitchen each turn** (Special Delivery, Dodgy Inspector, Breeder, Poach). The rat you draw doesn't count.

---

## THE INSPECTORS

Every inspection compares its **colour** to the rats in the target's kitchen. Damage equals the total **heat** of matching rats. Wrong colour, no rats of it — nothing happens.

| Card | Copies | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Health Inspection (Naked Mole Rat)** | 3 | Play it on any rival. Flat **−1 star**, immediately. Ignores colour |
| **Scheduled Inspection — 1 colour** | 6 | **Arm it** on your turn. On a **later** turn, fire it at a rival |
| **Scheduled Inspection — 2 colour** | 6 | Same, but printed with two colours — **choose one** when you fire |
| **Snap Inspection** | 6 | **Fires on you the moment you draw it.** One colour. Cannot be held |
| **Snap Inspection (Mole)** | 2 | Fires on you when drawn. Flat **−1 star** |

### Arming
- Only **one** inspection armed at a time.
- It **cannot fire the turn you arm it** — you must wait until your next turn.
- It expires unfired at the end of that turn.
- You can't fire at someone **boarded up**, or a kitchen you've already touched this turn.

## THE ACTION CARDS

### Take, give and remove rats
| Card | Copies | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Special Delivery | 6 | Take any rat from the alley and put it in **any kitchen** — yours or a rival's. A rival's kitchen counts as touched |
| Dodgy Inspector | 3 | **Lose 1 star**, then take any rat from the alley |
| Poach | 5 | Steal a **weight-1** rat from a rival. You choose which |
| Ratata Quente | 4 | Dump one of your rats into a rival's kitchen |
| Hot Chilli | 4 | Place on a rival's rat. It goes to the bins at the start of your next turn |
| Exterminator | 4 | Remove any rat, anyone's, to the bins |

### Cards and inspections
| Card | Copies | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Grease the Palm | 3 | Destroy a rival's **armed** inspection before it fires |
| Kleptomaniac | 3 | Steal a random card from any hand |
| Gambit | 6 | Look at the top 3 cards. Keep 1, put 2 back on top |

### Protect and recover
| Card | Copies | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Food | 12 | **Spend 2: regain 1 star.** Also feeds rats (1 each) |
| Board Up | 4 | Your kitchen can't be attacked until your next turn |
| Cat | 3 | Shield one of your rats (not a Fat Rat) from the next attack on it |
| Wok Block | 3 | **Reaction:** cancel one inspection aimed at you |
| Last Resort | 4 | Eat one of your rats, regain stars equal to its weight |
| Territorial | 3 | A rival's kitchen can't receive rats until your next turn |

**90 action cards** (including the 23 inspections above).

---

## MARKET DAYS

One flips at the start of every round.

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Rat Release** | Fewest rats takes one from the alley. Tie → fewest cards. Still tied → they all draw from the deck instead |
| **Frenzy** | Everyone draws a card |
| **Blackout** | Everyone discards a card |
| **Pied Piper** | Everyone picks one of their own rats face-down, then all pass left at once |
| **Russian Roulette** | Everyone puts 1 card face-down. Shuffle in the spare Naked Mole Rat, deal one back to each player, bin the leftover unseen. Whoever gets the Mole is inspected |
| **Inspector's Day Off** | No inspection can be **armed** this round. Already-armed ones still fire |

---

## LOSING

Lose your last star and your stall is **shut down**. Your rats go to the bins, your hand to the discard.

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

- **Stars:** 4 · **Hand limit:** 7 · **Rat King:** start your turn with weight 6
- **Setup:** 18 rats in the deck, the rest in the bins, 3 face-up in the alley
- **Colours:** red, blue, green
- **Play as many cards as you like** — but one touch per rival kitchen per turn
- **Two Food = one star.** One Food = feed a rat
- **You always draw one card a turn** — it just might be a rat, or an inspector

---

*Rats can't be killed. They only ever move somewhere else.*
